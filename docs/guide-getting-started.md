# Getting started with OtakuSoul

[🇬🇧 English](guide-getting-started.md) • [🇩🇪 Deutsch](guide-getting-started.de.md) • [🇷🇺 Русский](guide-getting-started.ru.md)

OtakuSoul is an app for conversations and stories with AI characters. This guide takes you to the first reply in a
few minutes – no prior knowledge of AI models needed.

> Applies from OtakuSoul 0.5.0. Unfamiliar terms are explained in the app's **glossary** (question mark at the top
> right → "Glossary").

## 1. Set up the AI

On the first start an assistant opens. In the **"AI model"** step you choose:

- **Local** – the AI runs on your graphics card, private and free. Click **"Set everything up automatically"**: the
  app downloads the matching AI runtime and a recommended model and selects it. The traffic light next to each model
  shows whether it fits your graphics memory (*fits*, *tight*, *too big*).
- **Cloud** – a provider computes for you. Pick a provider card, follow **"Where do I get a key?"**, paste the API
  key and click **"Test connection"**.

The app recommends one of the two depending on your graphics card. Later you can change everything on the **start
page** under **"Change AI"**.

## 2. Pick a character

**Characters** holds the characters. The bundled ones carry **"Good to start with"**. Clicking the picture shows who
the character is and how your chat begins; **"Chat now"** starts the conversation.

- More characters are in the **hub**.
- Create your own with **"Create character"** – an assistant guides you through concept, looks and personality.

## 3. Chatting

- Just start writing. Text between asterisks (`*smiles*`) is an action.
- Don't like a reply? Have it rewritten – the old one stays as a variant.
- Stuck for words? The **light bulb** next to the message field ("What could I say?") suggests three replies – a
  click puts one into the field, where you can adjust it before sending.
- The **pen** ("Write for me") writes your next message. If the field already holds notes (e.g. "ask for the way"), it
  turns them into a message; "Undo" brings the notes back.
- The **clapperboard** ("Direction") tells the AI something outside the story, e.g. "Let it rain". Directions are
  written in `(( ))` – you can also type them directly – and stay out of the memory.
- Replies always sound the same? Turn on "Write with more variety" under **Settings → Reply style → Variety**. If the
  AI copies one of its last replies, the app rewrites it automatically.
- Below the message field you choose the **reply length** for this chat: short, medium or detailed.
- Tired of the AI's pet phrases? Add them under **Settings → Reply style → Words & phrases**.
- The character remembers your conversation. **"Conversation memory"** below the input shows how full it is; the app
  summarises older messages when needed.

## 4. Experience stories

In **Stage** a game master tells a story with several characters.

1. Pick a story in the **story gallery** – "For beginners" shows the easiest ones.
2. The preview shows the situation, companions and first ideas; **"Start story"** begins.
3. Below you choose what your turn is: **Say**, **Do**, **Think**, **Whisper** or **Direct**. No idea? Click a
   **suggestion**.
4. **"Continue"** lets the story go on without you, **"Undo"** takes back a round.

With **"Your own story"** you build your own in four steps: genre, your character, companions, situation.

## 5. Voice and images

In the **settings** you set up speech under **"Voice"** and image generation under **"Images"** – locally or through
a service. **"Listen to and compare voices"** at the top of the voice section plays every available voice; "Use" picks
it together with the matching speech engine. Every setting explains itself in a sentence below it, and **"Reset to
defaults"** returns an area to its starting state at any time.

## 6. When something goes wrong

- Many error messages offer a **fix button**, such as "Check API key" or "Choose a smaller model".
- Technical fine-tuning (server tuning, sampler values, prompt) appears with **expert mode** at the top of the settings.
- Questions and bug reports: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SnowwhiteOakheart/OtakuSoul/issues).
